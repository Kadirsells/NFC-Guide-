FAQ about NFC review cards

What is an NFC review card?

A card or stand with an NFC chip. The chip stores the link to a shop’s Google review form. When a customer holds their phone to it, the Google review form opens directly.

How do I program an NFC card with the Google review link?

You write the shop’s Google review link to the card’s NFC chip using your smartphone. Chapter 4 shows you how, step by step.

Do I need any prior knowledge?

No. The guide explains step by step how to buy, program and sell the cards to shops.

What does it cost to get started?

Besides the guide you need about €50 for your first cards and a business registration (in Germany roughly €20 to €60, depending on the municipality).

Do the cards work with every phone?

With all current iPhones and Android phones with NFC. For older phones you can add a QR code to the card.

Do I need a registered business?

If you sell cards regularly, yes. Chapter 2 explains the registration in Germany. This is not tax or legal advice.
